Privacy Policy

Kreox Growth Solution · Updated 25 November 2026

Your privacy matters. Here is how this website works and how information shared through a project enquiry is handled.

01

Introduction

This policy explains how Kreox Growth Solution handles information relating to this website and project enquiries. It describes the current website implementation and the information you choose to share with us.

02

Information You Share

  • The enquiry form asks for your name, email address, service requirements, and project message. Business name and phone number are optional.
  • If you send an email, we receive the message, your email address, and any attachments or other information you include. Please avoid sending passwords, payment details, or sensitive personal information through the enquiry form.
03

How the Enquiry Form Works

  • The form does not submit your enquiry to an agency database or form delivery provider. It prepares a Gmail compose window or an email-app draft using the details you enter.
  • Opening the Gmail draft passes those details to Gmail, where Google’s privacy terms apply. Nothing is emailed to us until you review the draft and press Send in your email service.
04

Use of Information

  • Information received by email may be used to reply to your enquiry, understand your requirements, discuss a proposal, and communicate about a project.
  • Any further handling of client records or project material should be described in the relevant client agreement.
05

Third-Party Services & Hosting

  • The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. The hosting provider may process technical information such as IP addresses and request logs when delivering the site.
  • Gmail or your chosen email provider handles drafts and sent messages under its own policies. Links to external websites lead to services with their own privacy practices.
06

Cookies & Tracking

  • The current website does not install advertising or analytics cookies, use tracking pixels, or store enquiry details in browser local storage.
  • Page effects, video playback, and form validation run in your browser. Hosting and email providers may use their own cookies or technical records when you interact with their services.
07

Security & Confidentiality

  • Public website traffic is served over HTTPS on the live domain. No method of communication or storage can guarantee complete security.
  • Please use authorised access-sharing methods for business accounts rather than sending credentials or verification codes through the website or by email.
08

Your Requests

  • You may contact us to ask about information you have emailed, request a correction, or request deletion. We may need to verify the request and consider any applicable record-keeping obligations.
  • Questions about information held directly by an email or hosting provider should also be addressed to that provider.
09

Retention & Policy Updates

  • The public website does not maintain a form-submission database. Emails and project records are handled separately from the website. Ask us about retention before sharing information if you have specific requirements.
  • This page may be revised when our website or data handling changes. The date below identifies the latest published version of this policy.

Privacy questions?

For questions or requests about information you have shared with us, email kreoxgrowthsolution@gmail.com.

How this website handles your project enquiry.

Contact enquiries

The enquiry form prepares a Gmail draft containing the details you enter. Clicking Open Gmail Draft opens Gmail with those details prefilled. You choose whether to send the email. No enquiry is delivered until you press Send in Gmail or your email app.

No form backend

This website does not submit enquiry details to a form delivery service or maintain an enquiries database. Opening the Gmail draft shares the draft contents with Gmail; the email provider handles that information under its own privacy terms. You can also use the email app option.

Information you share

If you email Kreox Growth Solution, we receive the information you choose to include, such as your name, contact details and project requirements. We use that information to respond and discuss your enquiry.

Cookies and analytics

This version of the website does not install analytics or marketing cookies. Your hosting provider may process technical request information to deliver the website.

Questions

For questions about your enquiry or to request deletion of information you have emailed, contact growveradigitall@gmail.com.

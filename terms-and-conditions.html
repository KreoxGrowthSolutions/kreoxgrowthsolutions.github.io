Kreox Growth Solution · Client Engagement Terms

These terms outline how our creative projects and digital marketing engagements are organised. Please read them alongside your service proposal.

Draft for review: payment, cancellation, refund, ownership, and other project-specific terms must be confirmed in your written agreement before an engagement begins.

01

Scope of Services

  • Kreox Growth Solution provides creative and digital marketing services, including Instagram page management, influencer collaborations, Meta Ads, branding, graphic design, video editing, and website design.
  • The accepted written proposal sets out the services, deliverables, revisions, schedule, and responsibilities for each engagement. Work outside that scope requires a separate agreement.
02

Page & Content Management

  • Posting schedules, content formats, approval steps, and responsibility for comments or direct messages are defined in the proposal.
  • Please coordinate independent posts and third-party collaborations with the agreed content plan. New accounts or significant account changes require client approval.
03

Models & Talent

Any models, presenters, creators, locations, or third-party production support must be agreed before a shoot. The proposal should identify who arranges them and any additional charges.

04

Advertising Budget

  • Advertising spend is separate from agency fees. Our published packages specify a client-paid Meta Ads budget of ₹500 per day.
  • Platform charges and other third-party costs should be confirmed before spending is authorised. Campaign budgets may be adjusted only by agreement.
05

Results & Performance

  • Marketing outcomes depend on the audience, offer, competition, budget, platform algorithms, and other factors. We do not guarantee a specific number of followers, leads, sales, rankings, or return on investment.
  • Reports and reviews are supplied according to the agreed scope.
06

Third-Party Platforms

  • Social networks, advertising platforms, hosting services, and other providers operate under their own policies. Their changes, outages, or review decisions can affect a project.
  • Any support for account restrictions or appeals is limited to the agreed scope; account reinstatement cannot be guaranteed.
07

Client Cooperation

  • Please provide accurate business information, authorised brand assets, required account access, approvals, and feedback within the agreed timelines.
  • Use platform access permissions where possible and keep passwords and verification codes secure. Delays in inputs may require an updated delivery schedule.
08

Account & Content Responsibility

  • Clients retain ownership and control of their business accounts. Each party is responsible for the material and information it supplies.
  • Clients should have permission to use supplied photographs, music, logos, footage, testimonials, and other assets. Content must comply with relevant platform rules and applicable law.
09

Shoots & Production

  • Shoot quantities and visits are limited to the selected package or proposal. Equipment, locations, travel, talent, and any extra production requirements should be agreed in advance.
  • Rescheduling, cancellations, and additional shoot costs are handled under the written proposal.
10

Fees, Taxes & Payment

  • Published packages carry a minimum commitment of three months. Quarterly and annual totals are calculated from the monthly rate, without an advertised discount.
  • The website lists 18% additional GST and a separate client-paid advertising budget. Payment dates, deposits, and any extra fees must be confirmed in the accepted proposal.
11

Cancellation, Renewal & Refunds

  • Cancellation notice, renewal arrangements, and refund eligibility must be set out in the accepted written proposal before work begins.
  • Any request should be sent in writing so completed work, committed third-party costs, and the applicable agreement can be reviewed. Nothing on this page limits rights that cannot legally be excluded.
12

Intellectual Property & Delivery

  • The proposal should specify ownership or licensing of final deliverables, when files are handed over, and whether editable project files, source files, fonts, music, or stock assets are included.
  • Third-party material remains subject to its own licence. Portfolio use or publication of confidential client material should be agreed with the client.
13

Privacy & Confidentiality

  • Information supplied for enquiries and project delivery is used as described in our Privacy Policy and the project agreement.
  • Our website enquiry form prepares an email draft; the visitor must review it and press Send in their email service.

Changes & Contact

  • Service-specific terms are confirmed in the written proposal. Any changes to an ongoing engagement should be agreed in writing.
  • For questions about scope, fees, or these terms, contact Kreox Growth Solution before starting a project.
